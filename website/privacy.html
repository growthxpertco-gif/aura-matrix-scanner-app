Privacy and terms

This website

It has no account, form, cookie or tracking of its own. The practice tools run in your browser and save nothing. The company that hosts the pages (GitHub) receives your internet address, as any web host does.

The app

The consultant app saves client details, readings and reports on the consultant's own phone. It has no account and no server, and does not send that information to Growth Xpert. It leaves the phone only when the consultant shares a report or saves a backup. A phone's own backup service may also copy app data, according to its settings.

The test version connects to GitHub to check for updates.

Your details

If a consultant holds your details, ask that consultant to show, correct or delete them.
